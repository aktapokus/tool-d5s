# Digital 5S — User Guide

Digital 5S is an assistant that organizes a messy folder on your computer for you. You describe what you want in your own words, it builds a list of suggestions, you approve them, and it carries them out.

## How to use it — 4 steps

**1. Pick a folder**
Click **Select** next to the "Folder" box, browse to a folder on your computer, and pick it.

**2. Describe what you want**
Type into the "Request" box as if you were explaining it to a friend. Examples:
- "Move old files to an archive"
- "Clean up temporary files"
- "Group Word documents into one folder"
- "Delete empty folders"

No technical jargon needed — plain language works fine.

**3. Click Analyze and review the suggestions**
The program scans your folder and shows a list of suggestions along with a **5S score** (a measure of how organized your folder is, from A to F). Each suggested item explains why it was flagged.

**Nothing happens automatically.** No file is touched until you approve it.

**4. Select what you want and apply it**
Each suggestion has a checkbox next to it — uncheck anything you don't want. You can use the phase filters (Seiri, Seiton, Seiso...) to view only certain kinds of suggestions. When you're ready, click **Apply Selected**.

## What is 5S, and what do the scores mean

This is a factory-floor organization methodology adapted to files:

| Phase | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Seiri** (Sort) | Finds unnecessary/old files |
| **Seiton** (Set in order) | Groups files by topic |
| **Seiso** (Shine) | Cleans up temp/cache files |
| **Seiketsu** (Standardize) | Measures folder structure consistency |
| **Shitsuke** (Sustain) | Tracks whether order is maintained over time |

You'll see a score from 0-100 for each phase, plus an overall letter grade (A-F). A low score doesn't mean "bad" — it just means "there's a lot of room to improve here."

## Made a mistake? Undo it

If you've applied an action and changed your mind, click **Undo Last Operation**. The program reverses the last operation — moved files return to where they were. (Deleted files cannot be undone, so it's worth double-checking delete suggestions before approving them.)

This button works even if you close and reopen the page — your operation history is saved permanently.

## Downloading a report

Below the analysis result, the **Download HTML Report** button lets you save the full report of that analysis to your computer, to review or share later.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the program change my folder without permission?**
No. No file changes until you explicitly click "Apply Selected."

**I'm worried I accidentally deleted an important file.**
Delete suggestions always come with a low risk (`low`) label and a clear reason. If you're unsure about a suggestion, just uncheck it before applying.

**I'm seeing a "connection error."**
This usually means the AI model (LLM) is busy or not yet ready — wait a few seconds and try again.
